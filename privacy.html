Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

WhereWePraying? ("the app", "we", "us") helps Muslims find mosque Jama'ah times and use Islamic prayer tools. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it.

What we collect

  • Anonymous device ID. A random identifier stored on your device so your prayer settings, journal entries, and progress can sync without requiring an account.
  • Account info (only if you sign in). Email address, and optionally a username, recovery email, and avatar, used for sign-in, Friends, and leaderboards.
  • Location. Used only to calculate prayer times, Qiblah direction, and nearby mosques. You can enter a location manually instead of sharing your device location.
  • Notification tokens. If you enable prayer or friend-request notifications, we store a push subscription tied to your device so we can deliver them.
  • Content you submit. Journal entries, Community Ideas posts/comments, and mosque photos you choose to upload.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How data is stored

Data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (D1 database, KV storage, R2 for photos). Sessions expire automatically after 7 days of inactivity.

Third parties

  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend — sends magic-link sign-in emails and, if you opt in, notification emails.
  • AlAdhan & Al Quran Cloud APIs — public prayer-time and Qur'an data providers; your location coordinates may be sent to AlAdhan to calculate times.
  • Stripe — only if you choose to become a Supporter; payment details are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our servers.

Your controls

  • Export your data anytime via Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Delete your account anytime via Settings → Danger Zone. This permanently removes your account, journal, and submitted content from our servers. This cannot be undone.
  • Turn off notifications or location anytime in Settings or your device settings.

Children

WhereWePraying? is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@wherewepraying.com.
