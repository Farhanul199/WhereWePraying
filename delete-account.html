Delete Your WhereWePraying Account

Permanently remove your account and associated data

⚠️ This action is permanent. Once deleted, your account and all associated data cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the WhereWePraying app
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete My Account"
  4. Read the confirmation message
  5. Tap "Permanently Delete" to confirm

Your account will be deleted immediately and permanently.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your email address and display name
  • Your user ID and account preferences
  • Any mosque claims or edits you submitted
  • Your account login credentials
  • Any saved settings or personal data

What's NOT Deleted

  • Mosque prayer times: Public data shared by all users remains available
  • Anonymized statistics: We may retain anonymized usage data for site improvement (cannot be linked to you)
  • Claimed mosque data: Mosque information you submitted remains live (but no longer linked to your account)

Data Retention

Your account data is deleted immediately upon request. We do not retain backups of deleted accounts beyond standard system maintenance (typically 30 days for disaster recovery only).
