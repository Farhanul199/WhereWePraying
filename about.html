Our Mission

Find out. Get there. Catch the Jama'ah.

"Where we praying?" — every Muslim has asked it. We want the answer to take seconds, wherever you are.

Why we exist

Praying in congregation carries a reward many times greater than praying alone. Yet missing the Jama'ah is common, often for a simple reason: you didn't know when it was, or which mosque you could still reach in time.

Prayer start times can be calculated. Jama'ah times can't. Each mosque's committee sets them, and they change through the year. That information is scattered across noticeboards, websites and WhatsApp groups. We bring it together in one place.

What we believe

  • The Jama'ah comes first. Everything we build should help you reach the congregation, not keep you on your phone.
  • Accuracy over guesswork. We get Jama'ah times from mosques and trusted sources, and say clearly when a time isn't available.
  • Free, with no ads. The core app is free forever. No ads, no trackers, no selling your data.
  • Privacy by default. We only use your location to find mosques near you. Most features work without an account.
  • Built for the whole day. Beyond mosques: Qur'an, Du'a & Dhikr, Guides and a Journal to support your everyday worship.

Where we're going

We're starting in the UK and growing worldwide, one city at a time. Our aim is simple: wherever you are, open the app and know where the next Jama'ah is and whether you can make it.

Mosques, get involved

If you run a mosque and want your times shown accurately, we'd love to hear from you. Email support@wherewepraying.com.
