The Mission

Find out. Get there. Catch the Jama'ah.

Why this was made

Honestly? I got fed up. This is what my phone looked like: a whole folder of Islamic apps. One for mosque times, another for the Qur'an, another for du'a, one for tasbih, one to track my salah, and a few more I'd forgotten I even had.

Every app did one thing, and switching between them got old fast. So I decided to build the one app I actually wanted: somewhere to find a mosque nearby, read Qur'an, keep up with dhikr, track my prayers and learn the basics, all in one place.

That's WhereWePraying?.

What matters to me

  • One app, not ten. If it helps your day-to-day deen, it belongs here.
  • Free, with no ads. The core app stays free. No ads, no trackers, no selling your data.
  • Your privacy. Location is only used to find mosques near you, and most of the app works without an account.
  • Get you to the masjid. The goal is to spend less time on your phone, not more.

Where we're going

Most of Europe is already on the app, and we're slowly adding more countries and more mosques as we go. It's being built section by section, so expect things to keep improving.
