WhereWePraying?

The Mission

A companion for every step of your faith journey, all in one place.

Why this was made

This is what my phone used to look like: an entire folder of Islamic apps. One for mosque times, another for the Qur'an, another for du'a, one for tasbih, one to track my salah, and a few more I'd forgotten I even had.

Why are so many apps needed?
There has to be a better way..!
Hmm… what if it was all one app?
All of these in just one single app!

Each app did one thing well, but moving between them quickly became tiring. So I set out to build the one app I actually wanted: somewhere to find a mosque nearby, read Qur'an, keep up with dhikr, track my prayers and learn the basics, all in one place.

That's WhereWePraying?.

What matters to me

One app, not ten. If it helps your day-to-day deen, it belongs here.
Free, with no ads. The core app stays free. No ads, no trackers, no selling your data.
Your privacy. Location is only used to find mosques near you, and most of the app works without an account.
Get you to the masjid. Know which mosque is closest, when the next Jama'ah is, and whether you can still make it.

Where we're going

Most of Europe is already on the app, and we're slowly adding more countries and more mosques as we go. It's being built section by section, so expect things to keep improving.
