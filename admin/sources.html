Or find mosques by continent

Runs "Find mosques" for every country in that continent, one after another — your own site allows 10 admin requests a minute, so each country takes about 7 seconds. Small ones (Oceania, South America) finish in under 2 minutes; Africa/Europe/Asia take 5–6 minutes; "every country" takes about 20 minutes. Keep the tab open and active (don't switch away on mobile — background tabs can pause the timer).
