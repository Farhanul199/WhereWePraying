Common questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Prayer times, Qur'an, Du'a & Dhikr, Guides, Journal, and Travel Mode all work without signing in. An account is only needed for Friends, leaderboards, and Community Ideas.

How are Jama'ah times worked out?

Prayer begin times are calculated. Jama'ah (congregation) times are set by each mosque's committee, so we source them directly rather than calculating them — that's the whole reason the app exists.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This is permanent and removes your account, journal, and submitted content immediately.

How do I report an issue with a mosque's prayer times?

Use "Help improve accuracy" on the mosque's page, or email us directly with the mosque name and correction.

Is the app free?

Yes, core features are free forever with no ads. An optional Supporter tier helps fund development.
